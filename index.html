About Me

Hey, I’m Srini 👋


I’m a backend engineer with ~7 years of experience working on data-heavy systems across enterprise SaaS and consumer tech. I enjoy working on problems where requirements aren’t fully clear, data isn’t perfectly clean, and the system still needs to be reliable.


Over the years, I’ve worked on analytics platforms, AI-assisted email workflows, backend APIs, data pipelines, and privacy-compliant data systems. I care a lot about correctness, simplicity, and leaving systems easier to operate than I found them.


Outside work, I enjoy reading (non-fiction, philosophy, tech), lifting weights, playing badminton, and occasionally going down rabbit holes trying to understand how complex systems actually work.
